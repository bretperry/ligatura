TL;DR (2026-11-17, Waste Land re-run 2026-11-18; Mac mini M2 Pro CPU, transformers.js 4.3.2, `lab/exp123.sh`):
- **`--pairs` helps Latin and The Waste Land, hurts Hebrew and French.** Keep it opt-in; a per-language default needs more fixtures.
- **`--runs` helps The Waste Land most** (with `--pairs`, R@1 21 → 30). It costs R@1 on 5 of 7 fixtures. Keep it opt-in, for texts that quote in runs.
- **Fusing the reranker beats replacing it** on Kempis at pool 20 (R@1 34 vs 28), and on two more fixtures in Tuning below.

## What each flag does

- `--pairs`: a further RRF list. A target scores only if it shares 2+ stems with the source;
  score = ln(1 + Σ rarity / (gap of the two rarest in source + gap in target)), rarity = 1/df in
  each text (Tesserae). Votes with the profile's `words` weight. Same language only.
- `--runs`: a further RRF list of own fused score + the best fused score of source i±1 at target
  j−1..j+1. Eval now links the whole source with `only` set to gold sources, so neighbours are real.
- `--rerank-fuse w`: inside the pool, order by 1/(60 + retrieval rank) + w/(60 + reranker rank)
  instead of the reranker's order alone.

## Not yet tried

- `--runs` at a lower vote (0.5), or only lifting targets already in own top 100.
- `--pairs` with the Hebrew and French profiles inspected: proclitic splitting may make common
  stems look like pairs.
- Fused reranker on a second fixture (waste-land, augustine-vulgate) and at other weights.
